About Us

Where Every Cup Tells A Story & Every Page Finds Its Flavor

Welcome to Chai&Chapter, an artisanal haven born at the crossroads of slow-brewed tea rituals and literary discovery. We source premium whole-leaf teas, authentic organic spices, and specialty beans to craft drinks that comfort your soul. Pair our signature house-spiced Karak Chai or Kashmiri Kahwa with daily baked brioches and delicate pastries while immersing yourself in curated reads in our tranquil nooks.
