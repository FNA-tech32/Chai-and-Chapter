## 📖 About Chai&amp;Chapter

**Chai&amp;Chapter** is a boutique cafe and literary reading sanctuary designed for chai connoisseurs, coffee lovers, and avid readers. Combining the meditative tradition of slow-brewed spiced teas with the tranquility of a neighborhood book lounge, Chai&amp;Chapter offers an escape where every cup tells a story and every page finds its flavor.

From single-estate Assamese leaves, whole green cardamom, and Kashmiri saffron to freshly pulled brioches and velvet cheesecakes, the platform showcases an artisanal dining and cultural experience.

## ✨ Features & Architecture

### 🍵 1. Header & Dynamic Navigation
- **Sticky Header Transition:** Dynamically transforms on scroll with high-contrast background and subtle drop shadow.
- **Custom Vector Branding:** Scalable, retina-ready SVG logo (`chai-chapter-logo.svg`) featuring a chai cup and open-book motif.
- **Smooth Anchor Scrolling:** Animated viewport transitions to `#top`, `#about`, `#menu`, `#chefs`, `#offers`, and `#reservation`.
- **Mobile Off-Canvas Navigation:** Responsive hamburger toggle for handheld devices.

### 🌟 2. Hero Slider
- **Fullscreen Ambience Carousel:** High-resolution photography powered by Slick Slider with smooth autoplay and pagination.
- **Call-to-Action:** Direct quick-reservation button routing guests to the booking desk.

### 📚 3. Story & Reading Sanctuary (`#about`)
- **Brand Narrative:** Introduces the artisanal roots of slow-brewed spiced chai, book collections, and community nooks.
- **Visual Collage:** Showcase of tea craftsmanship, cozy corners, and fresh patisserie bakes.
- **Video Preview:** Interactive video modal showcase.

### 🥐 4. Artisanal Menu Carousel (`#menu`)
- **Responsive Multi-Item Carousel:** Owl Carousel displaying signature culinary items:
  - *Dark Cocoa Truffle Cake*
  - *Chai-Spiced Fluffy Pancakes*
  - *Cardamom Brioche Loaf*
  - *Wild Blueberry Vanilla Cheesecake*
  - *Masala Chai Cupcake*
  - *Royal Saffron Pistachio Tart*
- **Instant Reservation Links:** Direct one-click booking triggers for every specialty item.

### 👨‍🍳 5. Culinary & Beverage Artisans (`#chefs`)
- Highlights the talent behind the menu:
  - **Randy Walker** – *Master Tea Sommelier*
  - **David Martin** – *Head Pastry Artisan*
  - **Peter Perkson** – *Executive Beverage & Brunch Chef*

### 📅 6. Comprehensive Reservation & Contact Directory (`#reservation`)
- **Multi-Channel Contact Cards:** Dual-column grid displaying categorized channels with primary and alternative methods.
- **Interactive Reservation Form:**
  - Full Name, Email, and Phone inputs.
  - **Contact Channel Preference:** Guest selects *Email (Primary)*, *Alternative: WhatsApp / SMS*, or *Alternative: Direct Phone Call*.
  - **Experience Selection:** Tailored options for *Chai Tasting & High Tea*, *Quiet Reading Nook*, *Book Club Gathering (4-12 Guests)*, or *Casual Dining*.
  - Datepicker and curated time slots (*Morning Reads*, *Afternoon High Tea*, *Evening Literary Sessions*).
  - Client-side submission with instant confirmation feedback and form reset.

### 🍽️ 7. Weekly Specials (`#offers`)
- **Tabbed Experience:** Seamless tabs for Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner pairings with pricing, descriptions, and imagery.

### 🏛️ 8. Footer
- White vector logo (`chai-chapter-white-logo.svg`).
- Social media directory (Facebook, Twitter/X, Instagram, Goodreads).
- Direct address and contact summary.
- Official copyright notice.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1. Prerequisites
No build steps or complex compilers required. You only need a modern web browser (Google Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Microsoft Edge).

### 2. Running Locally

#### Option A: Direct Open
Double click [`index.html`](index.html) or open it directly in your browser.

#### Option B: Local HTTP Server (Recommended)
Using Python:
```bash
# In the project directory:
python -m http.server 8000
```
Then visit `http://localhost:8000` in your browser.

Using Node.js:
```bash
npx serve .
```
